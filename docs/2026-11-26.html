The U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis reported that the PCE price index reached 3.8% in October. This metric is critical because it is the primary gauge the Federal Reserve uses to set monetary policy. When inflation remains high, the Fed is more likely to maintain high interest rates to cool the economy. For investors, this creates a ’higher-for-longer’ environment, which typically strengthens the US Dollar and makes emerging market debt, including Egypt’s, more expensive to service. As the Fed prepares for its final 2026 rate meeting, the persistence of this inflation data complicates the outlook for potential rate cuts. Investors should watch for how this data influences the Fed’s rhetoric regarding future policy, as any signal of further tightening could lead to capital outflows from emerging markets like Egypt, putting additional strain on the EGP.

High inflation -> Fed maintains high interest rates -> Dollar strengthens and capital leaves emerging markets -> Egypt faces higher debt costs and currency pressure -> Watch Fed meeting signals.

When the pound weakens, the cost of purchasing foreign goods—ranging from fuel to basic commodities—rises in local currency terms. Investors in the Egyptian market monitor this closely, as currency volatility can affect the valuation of foreign-held assets and the overall attractiveness of the EGX. EPS

Earnings Per Share (EPS) is a company's profit divided by its total number of shares. It shows how much profit is allocated to each share of stock. For example, if an Egyptian company earns 1 million EGP and has 100,000 shares, the EPS is 10 EGP.

Investors look at EPS to determine if a company is profitable enough to justify buying its stock.

ربحية السهم

ربحية السهم (EPS) هي صافي أرباح الشركة مقسوماً على عدد أسهمها. توضح هذه النسبة مقدار الربح المخصص لكل سهم. على سبيل المثال، إذا حققت شركة مصرية أرباحاً بقيمة مليون جنيه ولديها 100 ألف سهم، فإن ربحية السهم تكون 10 جنيهات.

ينظر المستثمرون إلى ربحية السهم لتحديد ما إذا كانت الشركة مربحة بما يكفي لتبرير شراء أسهمها.
