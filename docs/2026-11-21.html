Wall Street suffered a severe sell-off on Friday, dragging global equity markets down as technology stocks experienced their sharpest decline in years. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite plunged 5.28%, while the S&P 500 fell 3.70% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 1.93%, placing both indices into correction territory—defined as a 10% drop from recent high levels. A primary trigger for the market rout was a deepening slump in chipmaker Nvidia, whose stock plunged 14% amid an accounting investigation by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). Furthermore, the stock market debut of artificial intelligence firm Anthropic failed to restore investor sentiment regarding elevated AI valuations. The market shock extended across Europe and Asia, with Germany’s DAX falling 2.60%, Japan’s Nikkei 225 dropping 5.06%, and the MSCI Emerging Markets fund tumbling 5.13%. Volatility spiked dramatically, sending the Cboe Volatility Index (VIX)—often called Wall Street’s fear gauge—up 26.20% to 35.89. Defensive sectors such as consumer staples gained 0.67% as investors sought safety in stable dividend-paying companies. For Egyptian investors, the global decline in equity markets creates selling pressure on emerging market funds and foreign capital allocations.

Precious metals experienced a steep sell-off on Friday, with silver tumbling 7.41% to $41.03 per ounce and gold falling 4.62% to $3,515.40 per ounce. Copper also drifted lower by 2.63% to $5.48 per pound, reflecting broader market anxiety and liquidity-driven liquidations across global commodity exchanges as equity markets crashed. Conversely, energy markets showed sharp divergence. U.S. natural gas prices surged 4.38% to $3.41 per million British thermal units. The spike in natural gas comes as an early breakdown in the polar vortex threatens parts of Europe and North America with severe cold weather, escalating heating demand at a time when European gas storage facilities started the winter season at their lowest levels in more than ten years. Meanwhile, crude oil prices recorded modest gains, with Brent crude rising 0.64% to $92.57 per barrel and West Texas Intermediate (WTI) increasing 0.93% to $83.98 per barrel. For Egypt, higher global gas and oil prices increase the national import bill for energy, whereas falling precious metals reduce the spot value of gold reserves held by institutions and local households.

The Egyptian government has raised natural gas prices for domestic fertilizer producers by 50% while simultaneously granting these companies the right to export 100% of their production. Previously, fertilizer manufacturers in Egypt were required to supply a fixed quota of their output to the local agricultural market at subsidized rates. The policy shift allows nitrogen and phosphate fertilizer producers to fully leverage international selling prices, providing them with enhanced foreign currency revenues. This decision comes as Egypt continues to stabilize its external position, having recently repaid $38.2 billion in external debt obligations backed by foreign exchange reserves. However, the Egyptian Exchange (EGX) faced headwinds over the week, with foreign investor sell-offs wiping EGP 135 billion off total market capitalization amid global market risk aversion. The higher input costs for domestic gas present operational challenges for chemical manufacturers, but full export access allows them to generate U.S. dollars needed for raw material imports and debt service. For the Egyptian economy, the move balances fiscal reform with foreign currency generation, though domestic agricultural input costs may rise.

تعلّم لغة الأسواق

dividend

A dividend is a cash or stock payment distributed by a publicly traded corporation to its shareholders out of accumulated profits. The company's board of directors decides the exact payout amount based on quarterly or annual earnings results. Investors view stable dividends as a signal of corporate financial health and sustainable cash generation.

If Commercial International Bank (CIB) earns strong net interest income in Cairo, its board may pay out a dividend of 2 EGP per share to all equity holders.

أرباح الأسهم الموزعة هي دفعة نقدية أو أسهم يوزعها مجلس إدارة الشركة على المساهمين من واقع الأرباح المحققة. يحدد مجلس الإدارة قيمة التوزيع بناءً على نتائج الأرباح الربع سنوية أو السنوية. يعتبر المستثمرون التوزيعات المستقرة مؤشراً على قوة الشركة وسلامة تدفقاتها النقدية.

إذا حقق البنك التجاري الدولي (CIB) في القاهرة أرباحاً قوية، فقد يقرر مجلس إدارته توزيع أرباح بقيمة 2 جنيه لكل سهم للمساهمين.

insolvency

Insolvency describes a financial state where a company cannot generate enough cash to pay its debt obligations when they become due. Rising interest rates worsen corporate insolvency risks by making debt refinancing significantly more expensive. Unresolved insolvency typically forces companies into legal bankruptcy proceedings or total liquidation.

If a Egyptian real estate firm faces high borrowing costs and low buyer sales, it may enter insolvency if it cannot repay its short-term bank loans.

تعسر السداد / الإفلاس

يعبر تعسر السداد عن حالة مالية تعجز فيها الشركة عن توليد نقدية كافية لسداد التزامات ديونها في مواعيد استحقاقها. وتؤدي الفائدة المرتفعة إلى زيادة مخاطر التعسر عبر جعل إعادة تمويل الديون أكثر تكلفة. يؤدي التعسر غير المعالج إلى اللجوء للقضاء لإشهار الإفلاس أو التصفية.

إذا واجهت شركة تطوير عقاري مصرية تكاليف اقتراض مرتفعة مع تراجع المبيعات، فقد تدخل في حالة تعسر إذا عجزت عن سداد قروضها البنكية قصيرة الأجل.

liquidation

Liquidation is the rapid selling of investment assets or corporate inventory to convert them into immediate cash reserves. In financial markets, liquidation occurs when traders panic or face margin calls, leading to steep drop in commodity and equity prices. In legal context, liquidation means closing a business completely and selling assets to pay off creditors.

When gold dropped 4.62% in global trading, Egyptian fund managers engaged in portfolio liquidation to maintain cash buffers.
