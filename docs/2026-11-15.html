US technology equities experienced a sharp decline on Friday, with the SPDR technology sector fund dropping 2.01% to 170.28 and the tech-heavy Nasdaq falling 0.91% to 25,136.27. The sector sell-off occurred as exchange-traded fund (ETF) experts highlighted how turmoil surrounding chipmaker Nvidia, alongside a Democratic-controlled Congress, could reshape the broader artificial intelligence (AI) trade. Analysts indicated that corporate governance uncertainty at major hardware providers might redirect enterprise capital toward competing semiconductor firms and alternate Big Tech stocks. Meanwhile, broader market volatility rose, with the Cboe Volatility Index (VIX)—a measure of market uncertainty—climbing 3.21% to 19.30. In contrast, defensive sectors attracted inflows, driving consumer staples up 1.42% and communication services up 1.05%. The S&P 500 index slipped 0.40% to 7,561.67, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 0.10% to close at 52,347.55. For students of finance, market sector rotation occurs when investors sell high-valuation growth stocks to purchase stable income-generating assets during periods of heightened policy or corporate uncertainty.

Learn the language of markets

تعلّم لغة الأسواق

Hedging

Hedging is an investment risk strategy designed to protect financial portfolios against unexpected price swings. Investors buy an offsetting security or contract that moves in the opposite direction of their main trade. This limits total potential losses, though it may also cap total gains.

An Egyptian wheat importer uses financial contracts to lock in global grain prices, preventing sudden foreign exchange losses if prices surge.

التحوط

التحوط هو إستراتيجية لمخاطر الاستثمار تهدف إلى حماية المحافظ المالية من تقلبات الأسعار غير المتوقعة. يشتري المستثمرون ورقة مالية أو عقداً معاكساً يتحرك في الاتجاه المعاكس لتجارتهم الرئيسية. هذا يحد من إجمالي الخسائر المحتملة، رغم أنه قد يضع حداً أقصى للمكاسب الإجمالية.

يستخدم مستورد قمح مصري عقوداً مالية لتثبيت أسعار الحبوب العالمية، مما يمنع خسائر النظير الأجنبي المفاجئة إذا ارتفعت الأسعار.

Bond Yield

Bond yield measures the annual interest return an investor earns relative to the bond's current market price. When bond prices decline due to selloffs, their yields increase proportionally to reflect higher interest rates. Rising yields signal elevated market interest rate expectations and higher borrowing costs across the economy.

When US 10-year Treasury yields rise to 5.15%, the Central Bank of Egypt may keep interest rates elevated to prevent local capital outflows.

عائد السندات

يقيس عائد السندات عائد الفائدة السنوي الذي يكسبه المستثمر بالنسبة لسعر السند الحالي في السوق. عندما تنخفض أسعار السندات بسبب المبيعات، ترتفع عوائدها بشكل تناسبي لتعكس أسعار فائدة أعلى. تشير العوائد المرتفعة إلى توقعات بمستويات فائدة أعلى وتكاليف اقتراض أكبر عبر الاقتصاد.

عندما ترتفع عوائد الخزانة الأمريكية لأجل 10 سنوات إلى 5.15%، قد يبقي البنك المركزي المصري أسعار الفائدة مرتفعة لمنع خروج رؤوس الأموال المحلية.

Initial Public Offering (IPO)

An Initial Public Offering occurs when a private corporate entity issues equity shares to public investors for the first time. This transition enables companies to raise substantial capital from retail and institutional investors to fund business expansion. In return, the firm becomes subject to public regulatory disclosures and stock market exchange rules.

If an Egyptian logistics startup launches an IPO on the Egyptian Exchange, retail investors in Cairo can buy shares directly through their brokers.
