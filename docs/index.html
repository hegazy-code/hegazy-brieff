Wall Street witnessed a sharp divergence on Thursday as renewed doubts over artificial intelligence spending triggered an aggressive retreat from mega-cap technology equities. The tech-heavy Nasdaq tumbled 2.60% to 26,008.86, while the broader S&P 500 fell 1.21% to 7,663.43. The SPDR Technology ETF dropped 3.29% to 182.34, leading market losses alongside Communication, which fell 1.42%. Investors dumped expensive growth assets amid mounting skepticism regarding when heavy capital expenditures on AI infrastructure will translate into corporate profits. Instead of moving purely to cash, fund managers executed a pronounced defensive rotation. Capital rotated into value-oriented and defensive sectors that offer steady dividends and inelastic demand. Consumer staples gained 3.31% to 89.99, Utilities climbed 2.07% to 42.87, Health care rose 1.32% to 181.30, and Financials advanced 1.09% to 56.65. Simultaneously, a flight to safety in the sovereign debt market drove bond yields downward. The US 10-year Treasury yield dropped 3.75% to 4.95%, and the 5-year yield decreased 4.10% to 4.77%. For emerging markets and Egyptian investors, this tech reassessment signals tightening risk appetite globally, even as retreating US yields provide marginal breathing room for sovereign borrowing.

The Egyptian pound weakened significantly against the greenback, with the USD/EGP rate advancing 1.66% to 55.25. The depreciation comes under the weight of foreign capital outflows, regional geopolitical tensions, and elevated energy import costs. Concurrently, the Egyptian Exchange suffered intense selling pressure. The benchmark EGX30 tumbled 3.41%, wiping out 116 billion Egyptian pounds in aggregate market capitalization across the trading week as foreign and Arab investors offloaded equities. Heavy losses swept across leading Egyptian watchlist equities. Talaat Moustafa Group plunged 3.23% to 87.00 EGP, despite extensive news flow surrounding a massive state-linked housing deal. Industrial and export-focused blue chips followed suit, with Abu Qir Fertilizers declining 2.98% to 92.90 EGP and Elsewedy Electric dropping 2.87% to 106.94 EGP. The broader market experienced a synchronized decline, with smaller-cap indices such as the EGX 70 also recording steep weekly losses of 7%. For Egyptian finance students, this scenario demonstrates how foreign exchange pressure and macroeconomic headwinds directly translate into domestic asset repricing. Elevated dollar rates raise operating costs and debt burdens for local corporations, driving institutional portfolios to de-risk. Liquidity remains the vital condition for stabilizing market sentiment on the Cairo exchange.

Learn the language of markets

تعلّم لغة الأسواق

IPO (Initial Public Offering)

An IPO occurs when a private company sells its shares to the general public for the first time on a stock exchange. This allows the business to raise fresh equity capital from global and retail investors to finance expansion. After the offering finishes, the company's stock trades freely on the public market every business day.

In India, Reliance Jio launched a $3.2 billion IPO that attracted massive investor subscriptions on its final day.

الطرح العام الأولي (IPO)

يحدث الطرح العام الأولي عندما تبيع شركة خاصة أسهماً للجمهور لأول مرة في البورصة. يتيح ذلك للشركة جمع رأس مال جديد من المستثمرين الأفراد والمؤسسات لتمويل التوسعات. وبعد انتهاء الطرح، تتداول أسهم الشركة بحرية في السوق المالية بشكل يومي.

في الهند، طرحت شركة ريليانس جيو أسهماً بقيمة 3.2 مليار دولار في اكتتاب عام أولي شهد إقبالاً ضخماً من المستثمرين.

Defensive Sectors

Defensive sectors consist of industries that supply essential products and services like electric utilities, health care, and basic food items. Consumers continue buying these goods regardless of whether the overall economy is expanding or contracting. Consequently, investors move their cash into defensive stocks when higher-risk sectors like technology face heavy selling.

When US tech stocks dropped sharply, investors shifted capital into consumer staples, driving that US sector fund up 3.31%.

القطاعات الدفاعية

تتكون القطاعات الدفاعية من الصناعات التي تقدم منتجات وخدمات أساسية مثل الكهرباء، الرعاية الصحية، والسلع الغذائية. يستمر المستهلكون في شراء هذه المنتجات بغض النظر عن نمو الاقتصاد أو انكماشه. ونتيجة لذلك، ينقل المستثمرون أموالهم إلى الأسهم الدفاعية عندما تتعرض القطاعات الأكثر خطورة مثل التكنولوجيا لبيوع مكثفة.

عندما هبطت أسهم التكنولوجيا الأمريكية بحادة، حول المستثمرون أموالهم إلى السلع الاستهلاكية الأساسية ليرتفع صندوق هذا القطاع بنسبة 3.31%.

Treasury Bills

Treasury bills are short-term government debt obligations with maturities ranging from a few weeks up to one year. Governments issue them to borrow funds from domestic and international banks to finance state budget deficits. Investors buy them at a discount and receive the full nominal face value upon maturity.

The Central Bank of Egypt offered 130 billion Egyptian pounds in Treasury bills, with investor demand driving yields above 25%.

أذون الخزانة

أذون الخزانة هي أدوات دين حكومية قصيرة الأجل تتراوح مدتها بين بضعة أسابيع وسنة واحدة. تصدرها الحكومات للاقتراض من البنوك المحلية والدولية لتمويل عجز الموازنة العامة. يشتريها المستثمرون بسعر مخصوم ويستردون قيمتها الاسمية الكاملة عند تاريخ الاستحقاق.

طرح البنك المركزي المصري أذون خزانة بقيمة 130 مليار جنيه، وتسبب إقبال المستثمرين في دفع العائد لتجاوز 25%.
