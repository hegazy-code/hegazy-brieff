The US Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that the Producer Price Index (PPI), which measures the prices domestic producers receive for their goods and services, jumped 6.3% in September over the previous twelve months. This is the largest annual increase recorded since December 2022. The sharp rise in wholesale pipeline costs indicates that businesses face substantial production cost pressures, which are frequently passed on to retail consumers in final prices. Simultaneously, Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City President Jeffrey Schmid warned against viewing the recent oil shock as transitory, reinforcing market expectations that monetary policymakers may be forced to implement additional interest rate hikes. Higher interest rates make borrowing more expensive across the global economy to deliberately slow down spending and curb price spikes. Market benchmarks reflected this hawkish stance, with the US 10-year Treasury yield rising to 5.15% while crude commodities surged, with Brent reaching $112.00 per barrel. For emerging markets including Egypt, sustained high interest rates in the United States tend to strengthen the US dollar, raise foreign debt refinancing costs, and trigger capital outflows from local sovereign debt markets into risk-free US dollar assets.

Official data released by the Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) revealed that remittances sent home by Egyptians working abroad totaled USD 33.9 billion during the first eight months of 2026. In parallel, Egypt’s net international reserves reached a provisional level of US$ 57,348.2 million at the end of September 2026. Remittances represent one of Egypt’s most critical foreign currency pillars alongside Suez Canal transit fees, tourism receipts, and sovereign exports. The sustained inflow of hard currency follows structural economic and fiscal adjustments that stabilized the Egyptian pound, which traded at 54.59 per US dollar. The Ministry of Finance noted that these reforms supported Moody’s upgrade of Egypt’s sovereign credit rating to B3 with a positive outlook, alongside S&P Global Ratings affirming a ’B/B’ rating with a stable outlook. Credit ratings inform international bondholders and institutional investors about a government’s creditworthiness and probability of debt default. A fortified foreign exchange reserve position provides vital insurance against international commodity spikes—particularly crude oil, which rose to $112.00 per barrel—ensuring that essential grain and energy imports can be financed through official commercial bank channels without draining domestic dollar reserves.

Learn the language of markets

تعلّم لغة الأسواق

Dividend

A dividend is a portion of a company's profit paid out directly to its shareholders. Companies issue dividends in cash or additional shares as a reward to investors for holding their stock. Payment amounts are approved by the board of directors based on corporate net income.

If an Egyptian company like Commercial International Bank pays a dividend of 2 EGP per share, an investor owning 1,000 shares receives 2,000 EGP.

أرباح الأسهم

تعتبر أرباح الأسهم حصة من أرباح الشركة يتم توزيعها مباشرة على المساهمين. تدفع الشركات هذه الأرباح نقداً أو في شكل أسهم مجانية كمكافأة للمستثمرين على الاحتفاظ بأسهمها. ويتم اعتماد مبالغ التوزيع من مجلس الإدارة بناءً على صافي أرباح الشركة.

إذا وزع البنك التجاري الدولي في مصر أرباحًا بقيمة جنيهين لكل سهم، يحصل المستثمر الذي يمتلك 1000 سهم على 2000 جنيه.

Treasury Bills

Treasury bills are short-term government debt instruments used to fund government spending. They mature in less than one year and are sold at a discount to face value. Investors receive the full face value upon maturity, making the difference their earned interest.

When the Central Bank of Egypt auctions EGP 130 billion in treasury bills, local banks buy them to earn fixed interest returns from the government.

أذون الخزانة

أذون الخزانة هي أدوات دين حكومية قصيرة الأجل تُستخدم لتمويل الإنفاق الحكومي. تستحق هذه الأدوات في أقل من عام وتباع بخصم عن قيمتها الاسمية. ويحصل المستثمر على القيمة الاسمية كاملة عند الاستحقاق، مما يمثل الفارق الفائدة المكتسبة.

عندما يطرح البنك المركزي المصري أذون خزانة بقيمة 130 مليار جنيه، تشتريها البنوك المحلية لتحقيق عائد فائدة ثابت من الحكومة.

Remittances

Remittances are money transfers sent home by citizens working and living in foreign countries. They represent a vital source of national income and foreign exchange for developing economies. These transfers support household spending and national balance of payments stability.
