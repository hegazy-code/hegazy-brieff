Official central bank data confirmed that Egypt’s net foreign reserves climbed to $57.5 billion at the end of October 2026, marking a record level for the nation’s international liquidity buffers. Foreign reserves represent foreign currency assets held by a central bank to settle international balance-of-payments liabilities and support confidence in the local currency. In tandem with the reserve gains, Egypt signed a 5-year strategic economic agreement with the United States worth $625 million, cementing international financial backing. Following these announcements, the US dollar exchange rate against the Egyptian pound (USD/EGP) dropped 2.62% to 53.83 in local commercial banks. Expanded reserves provide critical insulation against external commodity price shocks, ensure smooth funding for essential food and energy imports, and enhance credit standing for future sovereign bond issuances. Market participants view the reserve build-up as a crucial stabilizing factor for Egypt’s foreign exchange market.

      Learn the language of markets

      تعلّم لغة الأسواق

      Exchange Rate

      The value of one currency against another. It determines how much foreign currency you can buy with your local money. In Egypt, the USD/EGP rate is critical for import costs.

      If the USD/EGP rate is 53.83, you need 53.83 EGP to buy 1 USD.

      سعر الصرف

      قيمة عملة مقابل أخرى. يحدد مقدار العملة الأجنبية التي يمكنك شراؤها بأموالك المحلية. في مصر، يعتبر سعر صرف الدولار مقابل الجنيه حاسماً لتكاليف الاستيراد.

      إذا كان سعر صرف الدولار مقابل الجنيه 53.83، فأنت بحاجة إلى 53.83 جنيه مصري لشراء دولار واحد.

      Net Foreign Reserves

      Foreign currency assets held by the Central Bank. These act as a safety net for the country's financial stability. They are used to cover imports and defend the currency.

      Egypt's reserves hit $57.5 billion, providing more stability for the Egyptian pound.

      صافي الاحتياطيات الأجنبية

      وصلت احتياطيات مصر إلى 57.5 مليار دولار، مما يوفر مزيداً من الاستقرار للجنيه المصري.

      Interest Rate

      The percentage charged by lenders for borrowing money. Central banks change this to control inflation and economic growth. Higher rates usually attract foreign investment but increase borrowing costs.

      The Bank of England raised rates to 4% to slow down inflation.

      سعر الفائدة

      النسبة المئوية التي يفرضها المقرضون لاقتراض الأموال. تغيرها البنوك المركزية للتحكم في التضخم والنمو الاقتصادي. عادة ما تجذب المعدلات المرتفعة الاستثمار الأجنبي ولكنها تزيد من تكاليف الاقتراض.

      رفع بنك إنجلترا أسعار الفائدة إلى 4% لإبطاء التضخم.
